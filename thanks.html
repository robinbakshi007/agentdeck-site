Getting your license key…

Keep this key: it's how your computer proves your subscription. It's also printed on your future invoices, and we've emailed it to you. Stripe sends your payment receipt separately.

Activate it

  1. If you haven't yet, download AgentDeck for Mac and open it.
  2. On your Mac, click the AgentDeck icon in the menu bar → Open dashboard, and paste the key under AgentDeck Pro license.
    Installed with Terminal instead? Run agentdeck-host activate <key>
  3. Pair your phone and you're done.
