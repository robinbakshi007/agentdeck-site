You're in. Welcome to Pulsar Pro

Three quick steps and your agent is in your pocket.

Getting your license key…

Keep this key: it's how your computer proves your plan. It's also on your invoices, and we've emailed it to you.

1

Install Pulsar on your computer

In Antigravity: open Extensions, search “Pulsar” and install it. Or download the Mac or Windows app.

2

Activate it with your key

In Antigravity, click Pulsar: activate license in the status bar. In the Mac app, click the Pulsar icon in the menu bar → Open dashboard and paste the key under Pulsar Pro license.

Installed with Terminal? Run agentdeck-host activate <key>

3

Pair your phone

Install the free app, then scan the pairing code your computer shows.
