Your AI coding agent, in your pocket

AgentDeck connects your phone to the Antigravity agent running on your computer. Walk away from your desk and stay in control.

  • See every agent conversation live, across all your projects
  • Send prompts, attach screenshots, or dictate by voice
  • Approve commands with Face ID or fingerprint
  • Review real code changes and diffs
  • Get a notification when the agent needs you or finishes
  • End-to-end encrypted over Wi-Fi or cellular

Get started

  1. Install the AgentDeck host on your Mac (one command).
  2. Install AgentDeck on your iPhone or Android phone.
  3. Scan the pairing code. Done.
