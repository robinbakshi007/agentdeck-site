Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026. Applies to the Pulsar mobile app, the Pulsar host software, and the Pulsar relay, provided by Finnova ("we").

The short version

Your code, prompts, conversations and files stay on your computer and your phone. We do not run servers that can read them, we do not sell data, and we do not show ads or use tracking or analytics SDKs. The only data we receive is anonymous crash reports, which you can turn off, and messages you send us.

What stays on your devices

  • Conversations, prompts, code, diffs, images and command output travel only between your phone and the Pulsar host on your own computer.
  • Pairing keys are stored in your phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) and in ~/.agentdeck on your computer.
  • Settings (theme, model choice, notification and Face ID preferences) are stored on your phone.

Data that passes through services

  • Relay (remote access). When your phone is not on the same network as your computer, traffic is forwarded by the Pulsar relay, hosted on Cloudflare. Traffic is end-to-end encrypted with a key only your phone and computer hold; the relay sees only a random host identifier, connection times, IP addresses and message sizes, and keeps none of the content. It stores a one-way hash of your host's relay token so no one else can impersonate your computer.
  • Push notifications. If you allow notifications, your phone's push token is stored only on your computer. Your computer sends notifications through Expo's push service and Apple (APNs) or Google (FCM). Notifications contain only a generic message such as "Approval needed" and a conversation identifier, never your code, prompts or commands.
  • Payments. Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details; we never see your card number. We receive and keep your email address, your Stripe customer ID, your subscription status and your license key, so we can tell your computer that your subscription is active. Your computer checks the license with our billing service about twice a day, sending only the license key and a random computer identifier.
  • Voice input. Dictation uses your phone's built-in speech recognition (Apple or Google). Depending on your device settings, audio may be processed on the device or by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. We do not receive the audio.
  • Antigravity. Prompts you send are processed by Antigravity on your computer under Google's terms, exactly as if you typed them there.

Crash reports and support messages

  • Crash reports. When the app or the host hits an unexpected error, it sends an anonymous report to our crash inbox (hosted on Cloudflare): the app version, the platform and OS version, and the error message and stack trace. Home-folder paths, email addresses and anything that looks like a key or token are removed before sending. Reports never include your prompts, code, conversations or file contents, and are deleted after 90 days. Turn this off in the app under Settings → Send anonymous crash reports, or on the computer by setting AGENTDECK_CRASH_REPORTS=0.
  • Support messages. If you contact us through the support form, we receive the email address and message you enter, and keep them for 90 days.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: only to scan the pairing QR code.
  • Photos: only for images you choose to attach to a prompt.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only while you are dictating.
  • Face ID / biometrics: to confirm approvals. Biometric data never leaves your phone's secure hardware.
  • Local network: to reach your computer on Wi-Fi.
  • Notifications: optional, for approval and task-finished alerts.

Retention and deletion

Unpairing a phone, or removing it from the host dashboard, deletes its key. Uninstalling the app deletes data stored on the phone. Deleting ~/.agentdeck removes all host data. The relay keeps no message content; its pinned host-token hash can be removed on request.

Children

Pulsar is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Your rights

Because we do not hold your content or account profile, most requests can be fulfilled on your own devices. For anything else, including access, correction or deletion requests under the Australian Privacy Act, GDPR or CCPA, contact us through the support page.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date. Material changes will be announced in the app.
