AgentDeck for Mac

A small menu-bar app that connects your phone to Antigravity on this Mac. No Terminal, nothing else to install.

Download AgentDeck.dmg

For Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 13 or later. On Windows? Download for Windows.

Already installed? Show my pairing code (open on that Mac).

Set up in a minute

  1. Open AgentDeck.dmg and drag AgentDeck into Applications.
  2. Open AgentDeck from Applications. A window shows a pairing code, and the AgentDeck icon appears in the menu bar.
  3. On your phone, open the AgentDeck app and tap Scan pairing code, or Enter code instead and type the 8 characters.

After that your phone works from anywhere, on mobile data or any Wi-Fi. AgentDeck starts with your Mac; to pair another phone, click the menu-bar icon → Pair a phone…

If macOS says it can't check AgentDeck for malicious software

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to AgentDeck, then open it again. (This step goes away once our Apple-notarized build ships.)
