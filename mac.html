Pulsar for Mac

A small menu-bar app that connects your phone to Antigravity on this Mac. No Terminal, nothing else to install.

Download Pulsar.dmg

For Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 13 or later. On Windows? Download for Windows.

Already installed? Show my pairing code (open on that Mac).

Set up in a minute

  1. Open Pulsar.dmg and drag Pulsar into Applications.
  2. Open Pulsar from Applications. A window shows a pairing code, and the Pulsar icon appears in the menu bar.
  3. On your phone, open the Pulsar app and tap Scan pairing code, or Enter code instead and type the 8 characters.

After that your phone works from anywhere, on mobile data or any Wi-Fi. Pulsar starts with your Mac; to pair another phone, click the menu-bar icon → Pair a phone…

If macOS says it can't check Pulsar for malicious software

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Pulsar, then open it again. (This step goes away once our Apple-notarized build ships.)
