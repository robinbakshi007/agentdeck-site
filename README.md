## Features (v1.3)
- **Multi-Agent Support:** Antigravity IDE, Antigravity CLI (`agy acp --stdio`, local or headless cloud Linux), Codex, Claude Code (with terminal hooks), Copilot CLI, Cursor, Goose, OpenCode, Qwen Code, and Google Jules.
- **Biometric Approvals + 3-Try PIN Fallback:** Approve or deny terminal commands with Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint — with automatic PIN fallback after 3 failed attempts, risk badges, and smart auto-approve.
- **1-Tap Screenshot Paste (Phone, Mac & Windows):** Paste screenshots straight from Samsung Keyboard / Gboard, recent phone screenshots, or screenshots just captured on your Mac or Windows PC.
- **Follow-Up Queuing:** Send prompts while your agent is busy; Pulsar queues them and dispatches them automatically when the current step completes.
- **Draft Persistence & 10-Min Lock Grace:** Switch apps without losing unsent prompts or re-authenticating every second.
- **Single-Completion Push Alerts:** One notification when a task finishes or needs approval (zero per-step spam).
- **Git Diffs & 1-Tap Git Actions:** Inspect file-by-file diffs, revert hunks, commit, push, stash, or open PRs from your phone.
- **Localhost Preview Tunnel:** Test `localhost:3000`, `5173`, `8080`, etc. running on your computer right on your phone.
- **End-to-End Encrypted (`XSalsa20-Poly1305`):** Works on Wi-Fi or 5G with zero port forwarding and automatic relay reconnect.
