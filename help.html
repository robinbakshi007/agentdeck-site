Getting started

How it works: 📱 Pulsar phone app ⇄ 🔒 end-to-end encrypted ⇄ 💻 Pulsar for Mac ⇄ Antigravity

  1. On your Mac, make sure Antigravity is installed and you're signed in.
  2. Download Pulsar for Mac, drag it to Applications and open it. A window shows a QR code and an 8-character code.
  3. On your phone, install Pulsar (iPhone & iPad on the App Store or Android) and tap Scan pairing code, or Enter code instead and type the code.
  4. Choose a PIN or fingerprint/Face ID lock. Your Antigravity conversations appear, grouped by project, and you can send prompts, watch the agent work, review changes and approve commands.

Installed the phone app first? On the welcome screen tap Send the download link to my Mac.

Common questions

Why isn't Pulsar just built on Antigravity's own Remote Control?

Remote Control is Google's private service, signed in with your Google account; other apps aren't allowed to use that login (Google has suspended accounts for it). So Pulsar uses the same idea in its own way: it runs inside Antigravity as an extension and talks to your phone through its own end-to-end encrypted connection, never touching your Google login.
