Getting started

How it works: 📱 AgentDeck phone app ⇄ 🔒 end-to-end encrypted ⇄ 💻 AgentDeck for Mac ⇄ Antigravity

  1. On your Mac, make sure Antigravity is installed and you're signed in.
  2. Download AgentDeck for Mac, drag it to Applications and open it. A window shows a QR code and an 8-character code.
  3. On your phone, install AgentDeck from the App Store or Google Play and tap Scan pairing code, or Enter code instead and type the code.
  4. Choose a PIN or fingerprint/Face ID lock. Your Antigravity conversations appear, grouped by project, and you can send prompts, watch the agent work, review changes and approve commands.

Installed the phone app first? On the welcome screen tap Send the download link to my Mac.

Common questions

Why isn't AgentDeck just built on Antigravity's own Remote Control?

Remote Control is Google's private service, signed in with your Google account; other apps aren't allowed to use that login (Google has suspended accounts for it). So AgentDeck uses the same idea in its own way: it runs inside Antigravity as an extension and talks to your phone through its own end-to-end encrypted connection, never touching your Google login.

Does my phone need to be on the same Wi-Fi as my Mac?

No. After pairing, AgentDeck works on mobile data or any Wi-Fi. At home it connects directly; anywhere else it goes through our relay, which only forwards end-to-end encrypted data it cannot read. Typed pairing codes work from anywhere too.

Does my Mac need to stay on?

Yes: the agent runs on your Mac, so it must be on, awake and online, with AgentDeck in the menu bar. To keep it awake while plugged in: System Settings → Battery (or Energy) → prevent automatic sleeping when the display is off.

Is my code sent to AgentDeck's servers?

No. Prompts, code and agent output go only between your phone and your Mac, end-to-end encrypted. See the privacy policy.

Why am I never asked to approve commands?

Antigravity only pauses for approval when its permission preset asks. In Antigravity: Settings → General → Permission Settings → Request Review (or Default, which asks before running anything outside its sandbox). With Turbo, commands run straight away and there is nothing to approve. Projects can override this under Settings → Projects.

Can I use it with Windows, Linux or an Intel Mac?

Yes. The Mac app works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, and there is a one-click Windows app. Linux uses the Terminal installer. See Download.

Can I pair more than one phone?

Yes. Menu-bar icon → Pair a phone… on your Mac. One subscription covers up to 3 computers.

I lost my phone

On your Mac: menu-bar icon → Open dashboard → remove the phone. It can no longer connect.

How do I update?

The phone app updates through the App Store or Google Play. When a newer AgentDeck for Mac is out, the menu-bar icon shows Update available, the dashboard shows a notice and your phone tells you. Download the new version from the Mac page and replace the old app.

How do I uninstall?

Mac app: menu-bar icon → turn off Start at login → Quit AgentDeck, then move AgentDeck from Applications to the Bin. Terminal install: run node ~/.agentdeck/bin/agentdeck-host.cjs uninstall.

Troubleshooting

Where is my pairing code? The website doesn't show one

The code is made by AgentDeck on your computer (it holds that computer's identity and a one-time key), so the website can't show it. On the computer where AgentDeck is installed: open the pairing page, or on a Mac click the AgentDeck menu-bar icon → Pair a phone…. Not installed yet? Download AgentDeck first.

"That code was not found"

Codes last 10 minutes. On your Mac, click New code in the pairing window (menu-bar icon → Pair a phone…) and type the new one. Check your Mac is online.

The phone says my Mac is offline
  1. Wake the Mac and check the AgentDeck icon is in the menu bar (open AgentDeck from Applications if not).
  2. Check the Mac has internet.
  3. On the phone, pull down on the conversation list to retry.
"Antigravity isn't running" / no conversations

Open Antigravity and sign in. Then menu-bar icon → Check Antigravity: it tests every part AgentDeck uses and says which one fails (useful to include when you contact us).

macOS says it can't check AgentDeck for malicious software

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to AgentDeck.

Work from the phone doesn't show in Antigravity

Update AgentDeck for Mac to 1.2 or newer. If an old conversation still looks out of date, quit and reopen Antigravity.

Billing and licenses

What does it cost?

The phone app is free. AgentDeck Pro for your computer is a monthly subscription: see pricing.

Where is my license key?

It's shown after checkout, printed on your invoices, and on the dashboard of any computer you activated. Lost it? Contact us with your billing email.

How do I cancel or change my card?

On your Mac: menu-bar icon → Open dashboard → Manage billing. You keep access until the end of the paid month.
