Questions

Will I be charged today?

No. You pay US$0.00 today. Stripe saves your payment method securely (we never see your card number), and the first charge is on day 30, only if you haven't cancelled.

How do I cancel?

From your account page in a few clicks, any time. If you cancel during the trial you're never charged. After that, Pulsar keeps working until the end of the month you've paid for.

What counts as a computer?

Each Mac, Windows or Linux computer where Pulsar runs with your key. Each one is US$0.99/month. Phones and tablets are unlimited and free. Add or remove computers any time from Manage billing.

Does the price go up after early access?

No. Early-access members keep US$0.99/month per computer for as long as they stay subscribed. New customers later pay the regular price.

Is the phone app free?

Yes. The iPhone, iPad and Android apps are free and contain no purchases. Your plan is only for the Pulsar software on your computer.

Prices and tax

Prices are in US dollars and exclude GST. Australian customers pay 10% GST on top, shown before you confirm. See our terms and privacy policy.
