Become an Android beta tester

Pulsar for Android is in a closed test on Google Play. Google asks new apps to be tested by real people for 14 days before they can go public, so every tester helps Pulsar launch. It's free while the beta runs, and you get the app before everyone else.

Join the free beta

Pulsar is free for beta testers. When the beta ends, you keep a beta-tester price of US$0.99/year per computer. We'll email you first, and nothing is charged unless you choose to upgrade.

That upgrade link isn't valid. Use the link in your latest Pulsar email, or contact support.

We use your email only for the beta and your beta-tester price. See our privacy policy.

Three steps (2 minutes)

  1. Join the Pulsar Android beta group with the Google account you use on your Android phone (tap Join group). You won't get emails from it.
  2. Open the tester page and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install Pulsar from Google Play on that phone, and keep it installed for at least 14 days.

Use the same Google account in all three steps. If the Play Store says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

Then pair it with your computer

Install Pulsar in Antigravity (Extensions → search Pulsar) or download the Mac/Windows app, then scan the pairing code in the phone app. No computer handy? Tap Try the demo first.

Found a bug or have an idea? Tell us in the app (Settings → Help & support) or at support@finnova.com.au. You can leave the test any time on the tester page.
