Download AgentDeck

AgentDeck runs quietly on the computer where you use Antigravity and connects it to the AgentDeck app on your phone.

Mac

Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 13 or later.

Download for Mac

Open the file, drag AgentDeck into Applications, open it. Mac setup help

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Download for Windows
  1. Double-click AgentDeck.exe. It installs for your user account (no admin needed), starts with Windows and opens the pairing page.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway (this goes away once our signed build ships).
  3. On your phone, scan the code or type the 8 characters.

Pair another phone later: open http://localhost:48632/pair. Remove AgentDeck: run %LOCALAPPDATA%\AgentDeck\AgentDeck.exe uninstall.

Linux

Needs Node.js 20 or newer. In a terminal: curl -fsSL https://agentdeck.finnova.com.au/install.sh | sh (details)
