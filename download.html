Recommended · Mac, Windows & Linux

⚡ Inside Antigravity / VS Code (Extension)

Runs directly inside Antigravity with zero background daemons to install. Works on macOS (Apple silicon & Intel), Windows, and Linux.

Open VSX (Antigravity) VS Code Marketplace Download .vsix
  1. In Antigravity, open Extensions (⇧⌘X on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+X on Windows/Linux), search Pulsar, and click Install.
  2. A Pair your phone tab opens automatically: scan the QR code with the Pulsar phone app.
  3. Click Pulsar: activate license in the bottom status bar to start your 90-day free trial or enter your license key.

🍎 macOS App

Apple silicon & Intel Macs, macOS 13+. Lives in your menu bar so your Mac stays reachable even when Antigravity restarts.

Download for Mac (.dmg)

Open the DMG, drag Pulsar to Applications, and launch it. Mac setup guide

🪟 Windows App

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Installs for your user account (no admin required) and opens the pairing screen automatically.

Download for Windows (.exe)

Pair another phone anytime at http://localhost:48632/pair.
