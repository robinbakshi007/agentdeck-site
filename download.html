Download Pulsar

Pulsar connects Antigravity on your computer to the Pulsar app on your phone. Get the phone app, then install Pulsar on your computer.

Get the phone app now: Android · iPhone & iPad beta. The App Store and Google Play versions are in review.

1. Get the phone app

Android (Android 7 or later): available now.

Download Pulsar for Android
  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap the button. The download is about 40 MB.
  2. Open Pulsar.apk. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (Settings shows this once), then tap Install.
  3. Open Pulsar and tap Scan pairing code, or Try the demo first.

This is the same app we have submitted to Google Play. When the Play version is approved, uninstall this one and install from Play to get automatic updates (just pair again).

iPhone and iPad: public beta through Apple's TestFlight (the App Store version is in review).

Join the iPhone & iPad beta
  1. On your iPhone or iPad, tap the button. It opens Apple's free TestFlight app (install it from the App Store if asked).
  2. Tap Accept, then Install. Open Pulsar and scan your pairing code, or try the demo.

Apple is checking the beta right now (usually within a day). If the link says the beta isn't accepting testers yet, leave your email and we'll tell you the moment it opens.

2. Install Pulsar on your computer

Already installed Pulsar on this computer? Your pairing code is made by Pulsar itself, not by this website.

Show my pairing code

Open this on the computer where Pulsar is installed. On a Mac you can also click the Pulsar menu-bar icon → Pair a phone…

Recommended: inside Antigravity

Like Antigravity's own Remote Control, Pulsar can run inside Antigravity: nothing else to install, and it works on Mac (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux.

Download the Antigravity extension

Easiest: in Antigravity open Extensions, search Pulsar and click Install (Open VSX). Or download the file and install it by hand:

  1. In Antigravity, open Extensions (⇧⌘X on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+X on Windows/Linux).
  2. Click the ··· menu at the top of the Extensions panel → Install from VSIX… → choose pulsar-antigravity.vsix.
  3. A Pair your phone tab opens: scan it with the Pulsar app. Later: the Pulsar item in the status bar.

Pulsar then runs whenever Antigravity is open (which is when your agents work). Prefer it always on? Use the Mac or Windows app below instead; they work together.

Mac

Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 13 or later.

Download for Mac

Open the file, drag Pulsar into Applications, open it. Mac setup help

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Download for Windows
  1. Double-click Pulsar.exe. It installs for your user account (no admin needed), starts with Windows and opens the pairing page.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway (this goes away once our signed build ships).
  3. On your phone, scan the code or type the 8 characters.

Pair another phone later: open http://localhost:48632/pair. Remove Pulsar: run %LOCALAPPDATA%\AgentDeck\AgentDeck.exe uninstall.

Linux

Needs Node.js 20 or newer. In a terminal: curl -fsSL https://agentdeck.finnova.com.au/install.sh | sh (details)
