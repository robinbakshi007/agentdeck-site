How Cloud Security Works

Your cloud VM connects outbound to wss://agentdeck-relay.finnova.com.au — no public ports, no SSH exposure, and no VPN needed. Every prompt, diff, and tool approval is end-to-end encrypted (XSalsa20-Poly1305) directly between your phone and your cloud container.
